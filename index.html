🌿 Na’s Philosophy of Flavor — Balance

I like everything to balance out. I don’t want one ingredient overpowering everything else. Sometimes when I taste other people’s cooking, it seems like one flavor just runs right over the top of everything else.

To me, good cooking is about bringing flavors together so they complement one another. I want to taste the different ingredients, not have one drown out all the rest.

Immersion blender — watch that bowl size!

Use a deep, roomy bowl or container. If it's too small or shallow, what you're mixing can fly everywhere! Keep the blending head submerged while it's running.

💗 Na’s Take on Oregano

I don’t use oregano in my Italian cooking. I find it overpowering, and even when I eat at Italian restaurants, I can tell when they’ve used too much. To me, it takes away from the other wonderful flavors.

I prefer sweet basil, tulsi basil, rosemary, marjoram, and other herbs, depending on what I’m making. Those are alternatives, not herbs I’m suggesting alongside oregano.

🌿 Good to know: two different oreganos

Mexican oregano and Mediterranean oregano come from different plants and can taste different. Mexican oregano is often citrusy and earthy; Mediterranean oregano is often peppery and herbal.

🌱 Na’s Tulsi Garden

I grew nine tulsi basil plants. They took off, grew bushy, and the smell walking by the garden was amazing!

Na’s tip: Tulsi is beautifully fragrant, but it can overpower a dish, too. Use it carefully and keep the flavors balanced.
